Our Story

Where Great Food Meets Great Moments.

Savora began with a simple idea: create a place where exceptional food and unforgettable moments could come together.

Founded in 2018 by Amina Yusuf, Savora started as a small family-inspired restaurant built around a love for fresh ingredients, bold flavors, and bringing people together around the table.

What began as a humble idea has grown into a restaurant where traditional flavors meet modern creativity. While we've grown over the years, our passion for great food and genuine hospitality has remained the same.

👩‍🍳

Lawal Bashir

Founder & Creative Director

Our Journey

From a Small Idea to Savora.

2018

The Beginning

Savora opens its doors as a small family-inspired restaurant founded by Amina Yusuf, with a simple goal: create memorable meals using fresh ingredients and bold flavors.

2020

New Flavors

Savora expands its menu, introducing dishes inspired by different culinary traditions while staying true to its love of fresh, flavorful food.

2023

Growing Together

After years of serving the community, Savora has welcomed thousands of guests and become a favorite spot for meals, celebrations, and gatherings.

Today

Still Growing

Savora continues to evolve, bringing new flavors and experiences to the table while keeping the same passion and hospitality that started it all.
